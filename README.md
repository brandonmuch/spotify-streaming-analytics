# Spotify Global Streaming Analytics

A data analytics portfolio project exploring streaming revenue, artist performance and regional trends across 22 global markets from 2022 to 2024.

---

## Project Overview

This project follows a full data analytics workflow from data generation through to a business presentation.

**Tools used:** Python, Power BI, PowerPoint

**Dataset:** Synthetic, modelled on real streaming market dynamics including regional ARPU differences, genre preferences by market and seasonal listening patterns.

---

---

## Dataset

- 7,957 rows
- 22 countries across 5 regions (Europe, Americas, Africa, Asia, LATAM)
- 48 artists across 9 genres
- 36 months of data (January 2022 to December 2024)

---

## Business Questions

1. Which markets generate the most revenue and which are growing fastest?
2. Which artists and genres are driving streams globally?
3. How does revenue trend across 2022, 2023 and 2024?
4. Where is listener engagement strongest and where are people skipping?
5. What is the scale of playlist engagement across markets?
6. Which regions should the business prioritise for growth investment?

---

## Key Insights

- **Q4 drives the highest revenue** across all three years due to holiday season listening behaviour
- **Europe leads on ARPU** with premium conversion rates between 68 and 72%
- **Africa shows the fastest growth trajectory** driven by Nigeria, South Africa and Kenya
- **No single genre dominates globally** -- Afrobeats, K-Pop and Latin each lead in their home regions
